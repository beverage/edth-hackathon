14. **Changed by 181.** Name matching: canonical tokens (accents folded, st→saint, street/road→rue, church→eglise, castle→chateau), score = 0.6 recall + 0.25 precision + 0.15 contiguous phrase; good match ≥ 0.7 and within 0.1 of the best. An exact name match drops partial ones ("Châtelet du Château de Vincennes").
15. **Changed by 185.** A category narrows a shared name only when a category word is said outside the name (checked deterministically on the transcript), so "the château de Vincennes" stays ambiguous and "the Château de Vincennes metro station" resolves.
16. **Changed by 124.** A name said as only category words ("the church", "la mairie") is treated as a category search, whatever the model returns.
17. A name not in the gazetteer with a category falls back to asking among that category; it never auto-resolves.
18. Each candidate carries its own precomputed placement (pin, radius, heading), so tapping a choice needs no second server call.
19. Streets: pin is the point on the street nearest the last position; with no last position, the street's middle vertex with a radius of half its extent (max 400 m). Parks and the château use their own extent (max 500 m) as minimum radius.
20. "Towards" with no last position draws only a target ring, since there is no origin for an arrow.
21. **Replaced by 46.** A movement report does not refresh the last-position timestamp: the speaker's location is not newly known.
22. Mic is released after each clip so iOS plays the spoken question through the speaker, not the earpiece. Cost: a short mic start-up on each press.
23. Hold-to-talk with a 400 ms threshold; a shorter tap (or a release during the permission prompt) switches to tap-to-stop. Clips under 350 ms are discarded.

30. Gazetteer names now also keep `name:uk`, `name:ru`, `int_name` and a Latin transliteration of the Ukrainian name (official 2010 system), so "Khreshchatyk" matches where OpenStreetMap has no English name.
31. The name normaliser keeps any script: Cyrillic is kept as written (й and ї are not folded), only Latin accents are folded, and apostrophes inside Cyrillic words are dropped.
32. Ukrainian case endings: the extractor also returns `landmark_name_lemma`, the name in dictionary form ("Софійського собору" → "Софійський собор"). It is a spelling normalisation of what was said, never a new place. The resolver searches both forms, and two Cyrillic words count as the same if their first four or more letters agree and only endings of up to three letters differ.
33. Ukrainian (and common Russian) category words are matched by stem plus case ending ("церкви", "мостом"). "міст" (bridge) is matched exactly only, because "міста" means "of the city".
34. Streets are also indexed without their street word, because people say "на Хрещатику", not "на вулиці Хрещатик". A street and a station of the same name are then both exact matches, and the app asks which.
35. Same-name matches within 120 m of each other (or one inside the other), neither larger than 300 m, are one place: they fold into one candidate and the circle widens to the largest of them. Examples: Bérault station and Place Bérault; Maidan Nezalezhnosti station, square and street. A long street never folds into its station.
36. When no name matches and the spoken name includes type words ("станція метро Хрещатик"), the type words are dropped and only places of that type are searched, so "Gare du Nord" cannot land on "Rue du Nord".

51. A report can carry a stated distance (`distance_m`) and a compass direction of travel (`direction`, eight points), and a place can be north-east, south-east, south-west or north-west of the speaker (Alex, 20 Nov: "Yes, add it"). The model only copies a distance that was said and converts its unit; it never works one out from a time or a number of streets. A distance over 20 km is dropped.
52. With a distance, "200 m south of X" pins 200 m out from X's edge instead of the fixed 100 m, which stands when no distance is said. The circle is 0.4 × the distance, 50 m at least: one compass word in eight is good to ±22.5°, which is 0.38 of the distance sideways.
53. "200 m from X" with no side given keeps the pin on X and widens the circle to X's extent plus 1.25 × the distance (a said distance is taken as good to a quarter). A direction of travel in the same report means the distance was how far they moved, so the circle is not widened.
54. Dead reckoning: a distance and a compass direction with no place ("moved 300 m north", "300 m north of my last position") move the marker from the last position, however old it is. The circle is the last position's circle plus 0.4 × the distance, so it grows each leg, and the position is an estimate (≈, as in decision 47). With no last position the app asks for a place; with only a distance or only a direction it asks for both. The phone's GPS is still never read.
55. "Moved 200 m towards X" goes 200 m along the leg, never past X, with the last position's circle plus a quarter of the distance (50 m at least); without a distance decision 46 stands. "Moved 300 m east on rue X" moves the reckoned point onto that street. A place that cannot be found does not stop a move that states its distance and direction: it is reckoned and the log says the place was ignored.
56. The client now sends the last position's circle (`radius_m`) with every report, so a reckoned position starts out as unsure as the one it came from. `uncertainty_m` is unchanged and still only sent for estimates.
